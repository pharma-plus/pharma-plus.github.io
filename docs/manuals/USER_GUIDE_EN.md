# User Guide — PHARMA MAROC GOLD ENTERPRISE V2.0

## 1. First login

1. Open the app (mobile, desktop, or web).
2. Enter the **email** and **password** provided by your administrator.
3. If **two-factor authentication (2FA)** is enabled, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, etc.).
4. The dashboard shows today's key indicators.

> On first login, a password change is mandatory (`must_change_password`).

---

## 2. Dashboard

- **Daily revenue**, **sales count**, **average basket**, **low stock**, and **upcoming expiries**.
- Tap any card to open the related module.
- The sync banner shows connection state: `Up to date` or `Pending sync` (offline mode).

---

## 3. Point-of-sale (POS)

1. Go to **Sales** → **New sale**.
2. **Scan** the barcode (mobile) or search by name.
3. Set the **quantity** and, if needed, the **unit price** and **prescription number**.
4. Apply any discount, choose the **payment method** (cash, card, credit…).
5. **Validate**: the receipt is printed, stock is updated automatically.
6. Without a network, the sale is **queued** and synchronized later.

> Refunds require a **PIN code** (configurable).

---

## 4. Catalog and stock

- **Medications**: barcode (EAN-13), price, VAT, alert threshold, expiry date.
- **FEFO**: items that expire first are sold first.
- **Stock**: movement history (receipt, transfer, reservation, sale). Reservations are tracked per batch.
- Automatic alerts: stock below threshold and medications expiring within 90 days.

---

## 5. Purchases and suppliers

- **New purchase**: choose the supplier, add items (purchase price, VAT), the invoice is recorded and stock is received.
- The supplier keeps a full history: purchases, invoices, late deliveries.

---

## 6. Prescriptions and patients

- Record a **prescription** (items + dosage) with **progressive dispensing**.
- The prescription is automatically set to `filled` when all items are dispensed.
- Patient history (purchases, allergies, conditions) with proper access rights.

---

## 7. Employees and attendance

- **Employees**: profiles, documents (ID, diplomas), bonuses, evaluations, payroll summary.
- **Attendance**: clock in/out, lateness computed against 09:00, leave, schedules, monthly summary.

---

## 8. Accounting

- **Chart of accounts**, **balanced journal entries**, **expense categories**, **expenses**.
- **Cash register**: opening, movements, closing (discrepancy control).
- **Trial balance** and periodic **closings**.

---

## 9. Notifications

- Tasks and alerts: low stock, expiries, events.
- Mark as **read** or **read all**.

---

## 10. Settings

- Profile, password change, interface language (**FR / AR / EN**).
- Pharmacy customization (logo, colors, contact details, currency).
- User and role management (for the administrator).

---

## 11. Offline mode

The app stores sales locally and **synchronizes** them as soon as the connection is restored. The sync state is always visible.

---

## 12. Help and support

- From **Help**: create a **ticket**, track its progress, and chat with the support team.
- Read this guide or contact your administrator for any question.
